What this project needs to accomplish

Customers should be able to find an event, reserve a spot, or buy a product without calling the store. Staff should be able to manage events and see the information they need without asking a developer.

01

Find events and products

Customers can browse the calendar, read event details, search the approved catalog, and check availability on a phone or computer.

02

Register without calling the store

The site handles registration forms, confirmations, capacity limits, waitlists, and attendee lists.

03

Buy through Square

Product and paid-event purchases use Square-hosted checkout. Square records the order and payment.

04

Run the day-to-day work

Staff can manage events, review attendees, and see useful store information without developer help.

What we will build

The design phase comes first.

Design Innovations will translate the requirements into a sitemap, customer and staff workflows, responsive page layouts, and a visual direction for the store. The owners will review the core direction before full implementation begins, within the two consolidated review rounds included in this SOW.

  • Site map and content priorities
  • Event, registration, and purchase journeys
  • Staff event and attendee workflows
  • Responsive page layouts and visual system

Responsive implementation

Build the approved layouts for current desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers, with store information, approved policies, contact details, and clear navigation.

Events and registration

Calendar, detail pages, registration forms, capacity tracking, waitlists, confirmation messages, paid-event support, and registration history where available.

Online storefront

Approved Square products, categories, variations, prices, images, search or filters, availability indicators, and agreed pickup or fulfillment choices.

Staff administration

Secure access to create, edit, publish, cancel, and archive events; review attendees and waitlists; track status; and export supported records.

Messages and reporting

Registration and transaction messages, approved customer templates, basic segmentation or exports, and day-to-day event, customer, order, and registration summaries.

Launch and handoff

Production deployment, source code, setup documentation, administrator quick-start guide, one 90-minute remote handoff, and a 30-day stabilization period.

The site will connect to one Square seller account and the agreed Canton location or locations. Square remains the official record for catalog, orders, payments, inventory, customers, and loyalty data.

Project terms

Client access

Client provides authorized access to Square, domain and DNS records, hosting, email, analytics, and other required accounts.

Client content

Client supplies product and event information, policies, brand assets, photography, and approved written content in usable form.

Review process

Includes up to two consolidated review rounds across the design checkpoint and staging review. Material staging issues should be reported with reproducible details within five business days.

Stabilization

For 30 days after launch, Design Innovations will correct reproducible in-scope defects that materially differ from this SOW.

Acceptance

A milestone is accepted when its applicable launch checks pass and reported material defects are corrected. Minor issues that do not block normal use do not delay acceptance.

Payment data

Checkout is hosted by Square. The website will not intentionally collect or store raw payment-card information.

Third-party costs

Client pays Square fees and all hosting, domain, email, subscription, transaction, and provider usage charges directly.

Ownership

After full payment, Client owns project-specific custom code and deliverables. Pre-existing tools, reusable methods, and third-party components retain their existing ownership and licenses.

Catalog and content baseline

The project uses the approved Square catalog and Client-provided content in usable form. Bulk catalog cleanup, product photography, original product copy, and large manual imports are not included.

Changes and delays

Scope changes require written approval and may change the fee or schedule. Delays in access, content, decisions, or consolidated feedback move dependent delivery dates by a comparable amount.

Confidentiality

Each party will protect nonpublic project materials and use them only to perform or review this work, subject to any disclosure required by law.

Ongoing work

After stabilization, maintenance, content changes, enhancements, and work caused by provider changes require a separate agreement or written approval at the stated hourly rate.

Not included in this phase

Native mobile applications; live streaming; gamification; recommendation systems; a separate custom cart, tax engine, shipping-carrier integration, marketplace, warehouse system, or replacement point of sale; large catalog cleanup; formal legal, PCI, privacy, regulatory, penetration, or security certification work.

Service dependencies

Square and other provider approvals, API limits, outages, policy changes, and pricing are outside Design Innovations' control. Supported pickup, fulfillment, tax, discount, catalog, inventory, customer, and loyalty settings must be maintained accurately in the Client's Square environment.

Termination

Either party may end the project for an uncured material breach. Client may also end for convenience and pays for completed work and noncancelable commitments through that date.

Offer validity

Pricing and schedule assumptions are valid through October 4, 2026 and may be revised afterward if the project has not begun.
